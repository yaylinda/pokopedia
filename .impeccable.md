## Design Context

### Users
Pokopedia is for players planning Pokemon Pokopia while they play on Nintendo Switch. The primary context is desktop planning, with a secondary goal of adapting cleanly to phone-sized use later. Users want quick reference lookups, ownership tracking, habitat component checks, and help deciding which Pokemon should share a house.

### Brand Personality
Playful, precise, and direct. The app should feel like a polished game companion: colorful enough to make the roster enjoyable to scan, structured enough for serious planning, and practical enough to keep open beside the game.

### Aesthetic Direction
Desktop-first data-visualization workspace with a clean, colorful Pokédex tone: comfort-level grouping is the primary visual structure, Pokémon portraits are the main landmarks, and supporting stats stay compact. It must not look like a wilderness journal, expedition log, scrapbook, generic admin dashboard, neon game overlay, or marketing page. Use a bright light theme for quick scanning beside the Switch.

### Design Principles
1. Make the grouping answer the question immediately: region first, then comfort level, then Pokémon.
2. Keep Pokémon portraits and names visually dominant; habitat, skills, and favorites are supporting details.
3. Make planning explainable: optimizer results must show why a house group is good, not just output names.
4. Keep user data portable: localStorage is the working store, with import/export JSON for backup.
5. Design for desktop density first while keeping component structure ready for mobile adaptation.
