# Experimental defined neighborhoods

Current-roster breakdown for two defined neighborhoods in every region:

- **Litter loop:** every regional evo group containing a Litter Pokémon, plus one complete Gather evo group when available.
- **Water & Grow garden:** every remaining evo group containing a Water or Grow Pokémon.
- **Left over:** every evo group assigned to neither defined neighborhood. These remain available for habitat, aesthetic, favorite-category, and Linda Like clustering.

An evo group is listed only once. When a group contains both Litter and Water/Grow, Litter takes precedence and the group is marked **also Water/Grow**.
